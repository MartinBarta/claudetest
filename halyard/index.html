    Halyard is a sandbox. Every symbol, company, quote and fill is generated by a seeded random-walk model running in this page; the opening positions and orders are examples. Market orders walk the displayed book; limit orders fill when the opposite side reaches your price; stops trigger on the last trade. Buying power is 2× equity less gross exposure.
